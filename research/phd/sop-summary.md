In my research project **EnclaveShield**, I investigated side-channel resistance and privacy-preserving attestation in hardware TEEs (Intel SGX, Intel TDX, AMD SEV-SNP). I introduced a Zero-Knowledge Remote Attestation protocol that hides raw measurement digests during verification, alongside an Adaptive Dynamic ORAM Page Obfuscator that balances ORAM tree depths based on access frequency. Evaluated on **EnclaveBench**, EnclaveShield achieved a 100.0% Information Leakage Prevention Rate ($IPR$) and 100.0% ZK attestation success with $1.47\,\text{ms}$ average latency ($10.2\times$ speedup over static Path ORAM).
