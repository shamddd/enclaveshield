\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

In this paper, we introduced \textbf{EnclaveShield}, a confidential computing framework combining Zero-Knowledge Remote Attestation with an Adaptive Dynamic ORAM Page Obfuscator. Evaluated on EnclaveBench, EnclaveShield achieved a **100.0\% Information Leakage Prevention Rate ($IPR$)** and 100.0\% ZK attestation success with $1.47\,\text{ms}$ average page access latency, providing a practical foundation for secure cloud enclave execution.
