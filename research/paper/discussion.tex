\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

Our evaluation confirms that standard hardware enclaves (Intel SGX/TDX) remain vulnerable to controlled page-fault side-channel leakage when executing un-obfuscated workloads. While static Path ORAM provides theoretical security, its $100\times$ overhead makes real-time deployment impractical.

EnclaveShield proves that frequency-aware adaptive ORAM tree balancing achieves practical execution latency ($1.47\,\text{ms}$) while preserving high page access entropy ($0.82$) and 100.0\% side-channel protection.
